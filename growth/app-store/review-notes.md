# Invited validation pilot

TestFlight builds may show an invite-only five-set consistency pilot under Settings → Help test Evolv. Results-only sharing is the default. Photo sharing requires the tester to select individual photos after completing the test and confirm that selection.

Only after that submission completes, the participant may separately enable future progress contribution under Settings → Privacy & Data → Pilot data sharing. One mode shares future structured results with no photos; the other asks after every scan. This is future-only, excludes same-day extras and validation scans, and never uploads a photo without individual scan-level selection and confirmation. The participant can turn future contribution off or delete all shared pilot data without deleting the on-device timeline.
